BRAND VOICE EXECUTION GUIDE FOR "{brand_voice}":
- professional: Crisp, authoritative. Data-backed claims. No puffery. Respects the reader's intelligence.
- friendly: Warm, inclusive, conversational. Uses "you" and "we" naturally. Asks questions. Short sentences.
- bold: Takes a stance. Challenges the status quo. Uses power words. Is provocative but credible — never reckless.
- luxury: Every word drips with exclusivity. Focuses on craftsmanship, heritage, rarity. Zero mass-market language.
- casual: Raw, honest, unfiltered. Speaks like a trusted friend. Direct. No corporate jargon. Contractions everywhere.
- authoritative: Commands the room. Uses definitive statements. Establishes category leadership. Cites proof.
- inspirational: Uplifting, visionary, belief-driven. Speaks to possibility and potential. Emotionally forward-looking.
- empathetic: Acknowledges the struggle first. Creates safety. Then offers the path forward. Never preachy.
- motivational: Energetic and action-oriented. Creates momentum. Makes the reader feel capable and ready to act.
- playful: Witty, light, unexpected. Subverts expectations with humor. Avoids being cringe or forced.
- trustworthy: Calm, transparent, evidence-rich. Lets facts speak. Anti-hype. Reliable and steady.

GOAL-SPECIFIC CTA STRATEGY FOR "{inferred_goal}":
- awareness: Education first. CTAs invite exploration — zero pressure, all curiosity. ("Discover", "Learn how", "See the story")
- lead_gen: Value exchange. CTAs offer something tangible — a guide, a trial, a consultation. ("Get free access", "Claim your guide", "Start free")
- sales: Proof + urgency. CTAs drive direct action or booking. ("Shop now", "Order today", "Book a call", "Claim offer")
- retention: Exclusivity + loyalty. CTAs reward existing relationships. ("Access your exclusive benefits", "Upgrade today", "Renew & save")
