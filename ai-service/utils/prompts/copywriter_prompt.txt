BRAND VOICE GUIDELINES FOR "{brand_voice}":
- professional: Use precise language, cite data points, maintain authority, avoid hype or casual slang
- friendly: Warm and conversational, use "you" and "we", ask questions, tell short stories
- bold: Make strong claims, challenge conventional thinking, use power words, be provocative but credible
- luxury: Exclusive and aspirational language, focus on craftsmanship and prestige, avoid discounts or mass-market terms
- casual: Real and relatable, short sentences, use everyday language, be honest and direct
- authoritative: Command the narrative, use definitive statements, establish expert credibility, cite leadership

GOAL-SPECIFIC CTA STRATEGY FOR "{inferred_goal}":
- awareness: Focus on education and discovery. CTAs invite exploration with zero pressure.
- lead_gen: Focus on free value exchange. CTAs offer something valuable in return for contact info.
- sales: Focus on proof and urgency. CTAs drive direct conversion or demo booking.
- retention: Focus on exclusive value and loyalty. CTAs reward existing users and encourage upgrade.
