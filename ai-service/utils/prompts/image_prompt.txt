   MANDATORY 7-PART PROMPT STRUCTURE — follow this exact order for every prompt:

   1. SUBJECT + FROZEN MOMENT (most critical — first sentence):
      Describe a specific person doing a specific thing at a specific micro-moment.
      This is a photograph, not a concept. Not "visualization of efficiency" — a person, a moment.
      BAD:  "abstract visualization of AI-powered growth and efficiency"
      GOOD: "A 38-year-old South Asian CMO stands at floor-to-ceiling windows at dusk,
             expression shifting from concentration to quiet confidence as she reviews
             live campaign metrics on a thin-bezel monitor"

   2. ENVIRONMENT:
      Specific location, time of day, architectural or natural details,
      foreground element, background. Nothing generic.

   3. LIGHTING — choose one approach and describe precisely:
      - Authority/professional: single large diffused key light, Rembrandt shadow geometry
      - Warmth/approachable: large softbox camera-left, warm golden fill
      - Dramatic/bold: split lighting, rim backlight, deep shadows
      - Natural/authentic: golden hour window light, overcast diffused
      - Premium/luxury: low-key, deep shadows, single precise highlight

   4. LENS PERSPECTIVE — describe the VISUAL EFFECT, not brand names:
      (works with any image generator — Midjourney, Flux, Stable Diffusion, DALL-E, Firefly, etc.)
      - "medium telephoto compression, shallow depth of field, subject sharp,
        background dissolved into smooth bokeh"
      - "wide environmental perspective, dramatic foreground element,
        environmental context fills the frame"
      - "macro detail, pin-sharp product focus, extreme shallow depth of field,
        background abstract blur"

   5. COLOR GRADE — describe what the colors DO, not film stock names:
      - professional/saas: "cool desaturated shadows, electric blue accent highlights,
        crisp whites, controlled tonal range"
      - bold/startup: "high contrast, lifted blacks, punchy saturated midtones,
        vivid accent colors"
      - luxury: "rich deep shadows, warm amber midtones, low saturation,
        precise white highlights, premium tonal range"
      - friendly: "warm skin tones, soft highlights, slightly lifted shadows,
        natural honest palette"
      - authoritative: "near-monochrome, controlled muted palette,
        institutional weight, deep contrast"

   6. QUALITY ANCHORS (always include):
      "advertising photography quality, award-winning commercial campaign aesthetic,
      hyper-realistic, ultra-detailed, professional production"

   7. NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS (always last — never skip):
      "no text, no words, no letters, no logos, no watermarks, no labels, clean composition"
