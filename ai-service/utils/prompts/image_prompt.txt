Before writing any prompt, establish a unified visual language for the entire campaign:

1. VISUAL IDENTITY DERIVATION
   Map brand voice "{brand_voice}" to a visual system:

   professional → Editorial precision. Clean geometry. Deliberate negative space. Structured compositions with mathematical balance. Think Apple product photography meets Bloomberg Businessweek editorial. Surfaces are pristine — glass, brushed aluminum, crisp fabric. Nothing accidental.

   friendly → Warm, lived-in authenticity. Soft natural textures — worn leather, sun-faded cotton, morning light through curtains. Human imperfection celebrated. Candid documentary style. Think Patagonia catalog meets Kinfolk magazine. Every image feels like a memory you'd frame.

   bold → Kinetic energy frozen at its peak. High contrast. Strong diagonal compositions. Dramatic chiaroscuro. Think Nike "Just Do It" campaign meets Wired magazine covers. Images that grab you by the collar. Every element is deliberate provocation.

   luxury → Obsessive restraint. Negative space is a deliberate statement. Single points of light on precious surfaces. Think Tom Ford campaign meets Architectural Digest. Textures you can almost feel — Italian marble, supple calfskin, hand-blown glass. Less is infinitely more.

   casual → Unposed truth. Real light, real mess, real life. Photojournalistic instinct meets lifestyle warmth. Think Instagram's best candid moments elevated to gallery quality. The camera catches what's actually happening, not what's staged.

   authoritative → Institutional gravitas. Symmetry and weight. Classical composition. Deep shadows that suggest depth of knowledge. Think The Economist covers meets architectural photography. Power expressed through visual restraint, not visual noise.

   humorous → Unexpected juxtapositions. Bright, saturated, slightly surreal. Comic timing frozen in a single frame. Think Wes Anderson color palettes meets Old Spice campaign energy. The image makes you smile before you read a single word.

THE 10-LAYER PROMPT ARCHITECTURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Every prompt MUST follow this exact 10-layer structure. This is non-negotiable. This is what separates a $50 generic image from a $50,000 campaign asset.

LAYER 1 — THE FROZEN MOMENT & HUMAN CASTING ENGINE (30% of prompt weight)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
This is the MOST CRITICAL layer. The human character MUST NEVER be generic. Every human in the frame must feel like a real person specifically cast by a world-class casting director for this exact campaign — NEVER an AI stock model.

NO DEMOGRAPHIC SHOULD BECOME THE DEFAULT. NEVER default to a 40-year-old corporate executive in a blazer unless the campaign target is explicitly an enterprise executive!

MANDATORY 10-DIMENSION CHARACTER CASTING SYSTEM:
Every human depicted MUST incorporate ALL 10 of these character dimensions:

1. AUDIENCE-DERIVED CASTING (CRITICAL — MANDATORY BINDING TO {target_audience}):
   The character's age, profession, lifestyle, wardrobe style, environment, body language, and accessories MUST be derived directly from the campaign's target audience ({target_audience}):
   
   - Target = Gen-Z Students / Youth → Cast 19–22 years old, streetwear / casual layered fashion, campus / café / urban setting, natural unstyled hair, carrying backpack / phone / ear-buds, candid social or study moment. NEVER place in a corporate boardroom!
   - Target = Enterprise Executives / CFOs / CTOs → Cast 42–52 years old, tailored minimalist executive wardrobe, 32nd-floor corner office or modern tech studio, authoritative body language, premium accessories. NEVER place in a college dorm!
   - Target = Fitness / Wellness Enthusiasts → Cast athletic build, functional moisture-wicking sportswear, subtle skin glisten / sweat, dynamic weight distribution, high-energy environment.
   - Target = Young Parents / Families → Cast 30–40 years old, warm lived-in home / park setting, practical quality casual wardrobe, authentic unposed family interaction.
   - Target = Luxury Buyers → Cast 28–45 years old, high-fashion styling, bespoke fabrics (silk, cashmere, structured wool), architectural high-end setting, understated elegance.

2. DEMOGRAPHIC SPECIFICITY:
   Always state precise age + ethnicity/heritage + build + defining physical feature.
   ABSOLUTELY FORBIDDEN generic phrases:
   ✗ "a young woman", "a businessman", "a professional", "a person", "a model", "an executive"
   REQUIRED specific casting:
   ✓ "A 21-year-old Korean-American female sophomore with shoulder-length dark hair..."
   ✓ "A 46-year-old Latino chief financial officer with graying temples and sharp jawline..."

3. FACIAL MICRO-EXPRESSION & GAZE:
   Describe exact eye shape/tension, eyebrow position, mouth/lip posture, jaw tension, and gaze direction. Expression must authentically match the emotional beat of the scene.
   - "eyes narrowed in quiet intense focus, eyebrows slightly furrowed over the bridge of the nose, mouth set in a subtle determined line, gaze fixed on the code terminal 15 degrees off-camera"

4. HAIR & GROOMING:
   Specify exact hairstyle, hair texture, hair color, grooming, and facial hair (if applicable).
   - "soft curtain bangs framing her face, dark chestnut hair loosely clipped up with stray tendrils"
   - "close-cropped textured fade with a neatly trimmed 3-day stubble along the jawline"

5. WARDROBE PSYCHOLOGY:
   Wardrobe must communicate the character's persona and life story. Specify fabric texture, fit, garment colors, layering, and key accessories.
   ABSOLUTELY FORBIDDEN vague wardrobe:
   ✗ "wearing casual clothes", "wearing business attire", "dressed professionally"
   REQUIRED specific wardrobe:
   ✓ "an oversized washed-vintage charcoal hoodie over a crisp white crew-neck tee, silver chain necklace, worn canvas backpack strap across one shoulder"
   ✓ "an unbuttoned navy merino wool cardigan over an ivory silk blouse, vintage minimalist gold wrist-watch"

6. POSTURE & WEIGHT DISTRIBUTION:
   Describe physical standing/sitting angle, shoulder position, spine posture, movement, and balance. The pose must feel caught mid-motion by a documentary photographer, NEVER posed for a camera.
   - "slouched comfortably into a worn leather armchair, left foot tucked under her right knee, body angled 45 degrees toward the morning window light"

7. HAND MECHANICS (MANDATORY PHYSICAL TASK):
   Hands MUST ALWAYS have a precise, purposeful physical task. Hands MUST NEVER remain idle!
   EXAMPLES OF HAND TASKS:
   - Left hand holding a ceramic coffee mug by the handle while right index finger hovers over a laptop trackpad
   - Right hand adjusting silver reading glasses at the bridge, left hand resting flat on an open sketchbook
   - Fingers wrapped around the leather strap of a vintage camera, thumb resting on the shutter release button
   - Both hands mid-gesture explaining an idea, fingers slightly splayed, palms open at waist height

8. ENVIRONMENTAL AUTHENTICITY:
   The surroundings must naturally belong to the target audience persona. Match furniture, lighting, objects, and architectural language to the character's real world.

9. DIVERSITY WITHOUT BIAS:
   Systematically vary ethnicity, age, physique, and wardrobe across deliverables. Never repeat the same demographic archetype twice in a campaign!

10. CINEMATIC NATURALISM:
    The output image must look like documentary photojournalism, premium editorial campaign photography (Kinfolk, Vogue, Bloomberg Businessweek, Wired), or real commercial hero assets — NEVER generic AI stock photography!

REQUIRED OPENING SENTENCE PATTERN FOR LAYER 1:
✓ "A [exact age]-year-old [ethnicity/heritage] [persona/role] ([defining physical/hair feature]) is caught at the exact micro-second of [specific physical micro-action] — [facial micro-expression], [exact hand mechanic task], [what their posture and wardrobe reveal]"

EXAMPLES OF WORLD-CLASS AUDIENCE-MATCHED CASTING:

Example 1 (Target: Gen-Z College Students):
"A 20-year-old Afro-Latina college sophomore in an oversized vintage wash sage-green hoodie and silver hoop earrings, curly dark hair tied in a messy high bun with tendrils framing her face, is caught at the exact micro-second of burst laughter while leaning across a sunlit oak library table — her right hand holds a half-eaten matcha latte ceramic cup while her left index finger points at a shared laptop screen displaying a finished group presentation, her eyes crinkled with genuine unposed joy"

Example 2 (Target: Enterprise CFOs / Executives):
"A 48-year-old South Asian male Chief Financial Officer in a tailored unbuttoned charcoal Italian wool blazer over a crisp white open-collar shirt, subtle silver hair at temples and sharp groomed jawline, is caught at the suspended micro-second of decision — leaning forward against a dark walnut conference table at twilight, right hand resting flat over a printed Q3 financial audit while his left thumb and index finger adjust his thin titanium reading glasses, eyes narrowed in deep analytical focus"

Example 3 (Target: Young Parents):
"A 32-year-old Caucasian father in a soft sun-faded waffle-knit cream henley with rolled sleeves, short messy sandy-brown hair, is caught mid-giggle while crouching on a hardwood living room floor — his hands steadying a 3-year-old toddler taking shaky steps toward a wooden toy, his expression a radiant mix of pride and exhaustion, morning sunlight filtering through sheer white curtains behind him"

Example 4 (Target: Gym & Fitness Enthusiasts):
"A 27-year-old Black female track athlete with tight cornrows pulled into a low ponytail, wearing a matte black compression top with light sweat glistened across her collarbone, is caught at the exact second after completing her final rep — resting both hands on her knees, head lowered as her chest heaves, eyes closed in intense victorious satisfaction, warm gym spotlight highlighting muscle definition in her shoulders"

Example 5 (Target: Luxury Fashion Buyers):
"A 36-year-old East Asian female creative director with a sleek obsidian bob cut, wearing a structured ivory double-breasted silk trench coat, is caught mid-stride crossing a wet cobblestone courtyard at dusk — right hand holding a minimalist matte black leather clutch while her left hand gathers the collar against a light drizzle, her expression carrying the quiet, effortless confidence of old-world elegance"

LAYER 2 — THE WORLD (15% of prompt weight)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
The environment must be a CHARACTER in the story, not a backdrop.

RULES:
- Name the SPECIFIC type of space (not "modern office" but "32nd-floor corner office with floor-to-ceiling glass overlooking the financial district at twilight")
- Include at least ONE telling environmental detail that reveals character (a half-finished espresso, a dog-eared legal pad, a child's drawing pinned to a monitor)
- Specify time of day through light quality, not by stating "morning" or "evening"
- Include foreground element, midground subject, background depth — three planes minimum

LAYER 3 — ATMOSPHERIC TEXTURE (10% of prompt weight)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
What you can FEEL in the air. This is what makes images hauntingly real.

- Dust motes caught in a shaft of window light
- The faint blue glow of multiple screens reflecting off glass surfaces
- Steam rising from a ceramic mug catching warm sidelight
- Rain-beaded floor-to-ceiling windows diffusing city lights into soft halos
- The golden-hour warmth that makes skin glow and shadows go long and blue
- Morning fog through warehouse windows, diffusing hard light into ethereal softness

LAYER 4 — SURFACE & MATERIAL DETAIL (8% of prompt weight)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
What you can TOUCH. This is luxury's secret weapon.

- Describe 2-3 key surfaces: "brushed brass desk lamp, matte black laptop housing, soft grey cashmere sweater sleeve"
- Include fabric texture: "crisp white cotton shirt collar" or "worn denim jacket with faded creases at elbows"
- Material contrast: "cool marble desktop against warm leather portfolio" or "rough concrete wall behind smooth glass partition"

LAYER 5 — LIGHTING DESIGN (12% of prompt weight)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Light is your primary storytelling tool. Describe it like a cinematographer.

MATCH to brand voice:

professional / authoritative:
→ "Single large diffused key light positioned camera-left at 45 degrees, creating precise Rembrandt-pattern shadow geometry on the face — the nose shadow connecting to the cheek shadow forms a small triangle of light on the far cheek. Warm tungsten practical lights in the background at 2700K create depth separation. Fill ratio approximately 4:1, allowing shadows to hold detail without becoming muddy. A subtle hair light from behind camera-right separates the subject from the background."

friendly / approachable:
→ "Wraparound soft light from a large window source camera-left — imagine a north-facing studio window on an overcast afternoon. Gentle fill bounced from a warm surface camera-right lifts the shadows to approximately 2:1 ratio. No hard edges anywhere. Light wraps around the subject's face like a warm embrace. Skin tones rendered honestly — warm, dimensional, alive. Background receives natural light falloff, slightly darker, drawing focus forward."

bold / dramatic:
→ "Hard split lighting — a focused key light from camera-right casts half the face into deep shadow, creating a dramatic line of contrast down the center of the face. Strong edge light / rim light from behind and above separates the subject from a near-black background. Shadow fills 45% of the frame. Contrast ratio 8:1 or higher. The light is a weapon, not a comfort."

luxury / premium:
→ "Low-key lighting design — 70% of the frame lives in rich, textured shadow. A single precise spotlight illuminates ONLY the most important element (hands, product, eyes) with surgical accuracy. Everything else falls away into velvety darkness that still holds subtle detail. Light sources invisible, effect unmistakable. The darkness is as carefully crafted as the light."

casual / authentic:
→ "Natural available light only — golden hour sunlight streaming through a window at a low angle, casting long warm shadows across surfaces. Unmanipulated, honest, the way light actually falls in real spaces. No reflectors, no softboxes, no artificial anything. The imperfect light distribution IS the authenticity signal. Dust motes or atmospheric haze catching the light adds dimensional truth."

LAYER 6 — LENS PHYSICS & PERSPECTIVE (8% of prompt weight)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Describe the VISUAL EFFECT of optics, not equipment names. Universal across all generators.

Hero / flagship assets (email banner, landing page hero, youtube thumbnail, whitepaper cover):
→ "Medium telephoto compression effect — the background appears pulled closer to the subject, creating intimate environmental context. Shallow depth of field with subject's eyes in razor-sharp focus, everything behind the subject dissolving into a creamy, luminous bokeh. Subject positioned according to the rule of thirds, left-third, with intentional negative space on the right for text overlay. Foreground element slightly soft, adding depth layers."

Social posts — intimate (instagram post, facebook post, linkedin post):
→ "Medium portrait perspective, subject fills 55-65% of the frame. Shallow depth of field creates a soft, warm background separation. Tight, personal framing that creates the feeling of a one-on-one conversation. Background elements recognizable but soft, providing context without competition. Eye-level camera height for authentic connection."

Story formats (instagram story, tiktok cover, facebook story):
→ "Vertical portrait orientation with subject in the upper third of frame. Medium close-up framing, intimate and personal. Background compressed and softened to an abstract color wash that maintains mood without detail. Composition designed for vertical scrolling — the eye enters from the top and travels down naturally."

Wide establishing shots (webinar banner, blog header, google display ad):
→ "Wide environmental perspective with dramatic foreground element anchoring the lower third. Three distinct depth planes visible — foreground texture, midground subject, background environment. Atmospheric perspective adds haze and color shift to distant elements, creating natural depth. Full-width panoramic composition designed for horizontal banner formats."

Product / ecommerce & Demos (product showcase, product demos, comparison guides, feature showcases):
→ "Macro-influenced perspective, product/subject fills 65-75% of frame with pin-sharp detail on surface texture and material quality. Extreme shallow depth of field dissolves background into soft bokeh. Every surface imperfection, reflection, and material characteristic is rendered with obsessive clarity."

Testimonials & Case Studies (student testimonials, customer stories, case studies, interviews):
→ "Medium portrait perspective, 85mm telephoto compression, shallow depth of field with subject's eyes in razor-sharp focus, soft creamy background blur separating the subject from the authentic background environment while maintaining intimate visual connection."

LAYER 7 — COLOR SCIENCE (7% of prompt weight)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Describe color as emotional engineering, not technical specs.

professional + saas/tech:
→ "Cool desaturated shadows shifting toward deep blue-grey. Electric blue accent highlights on screen edges and glass reflections. Crisp, clinical whites in shirt collars and paper surfaces. Teal-shifted midtones in environmental surfaces. Controlled, precise tonal range — nothing accidental, every color choice deliberate. The palette whispers competence."

bold + any:
→ "Maximum contrast between light and shadow. Lifted blacks that retain texture but create dramatic separation. Punchy, saturated midtones that vibrate with energy. One vivid accent color that sears itself into memory. Colors don't suggest — they demand. The palette is a manifesto."

luxury + any:
→ "Rich, velvety shadows with visible texture deep in the dark tones. Warm amber shifting through the midtones like candlelight on mahogany. Deeply restrained saturation — color is rationed, never wasted. Highlights appear only on focal points like precious metal catching light. The palette has the quiet confidence of old money."

friendly + ecommerce/consumer:
→ "Warm, honest skin tones rendered with dimensional accuracy. Soft, diffused highlights that feel like afternoon sunlight. Lifted shadows — never crushed to black, always retaining warm detail. A natural, inviting palette that feels like coming home. Colors are approachable, never intimidating."

authoritative + finance/healthcare:
→ "Near-monochrome treatment with surgical color restraint. Deep, serious contrast that commands respect. Muted palette where color is an event, not a constant. Institutional visual weight built through tonal depth rather than chromatic variety. The palette carries the gravitas of carved stone."

casual + lifestyle:
→ "Natural, unprocessed color truth. Warm overall cast like late-afternoon film prints. Skin tones that look like skin, not like cosmetics. Slightly desaturated with honest warmth — the visual equivalent of a handwritten note. Nothing filtered, nothing artificial."

LAYER 8 — COMPOSITION & NEGATIVE SPACE (5% of prompt weight)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Design the frame for purpose.

- If text overlay will be added: "Intentional clean negative space in the [lower third / upper right / left third] of the frame — uncluttered background area with subtle gradient, designed to accommodate headline and CTA text overlay without competing with the subject"
- If no text overlay: "Full compositional use of the frame. Subject and environment fill the space with purpose. No dead areas, but clear visual hierarchy guides the eye from primary subject → secondary detail → environmental context"

LAYER 9 — QUALITY ANCHORS (3% of prompt weight)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Always include these exact phrases to anchor generation quality:

"Award-winning advertising photography, Cannes Lions Grand Prix quality, campaign-hero-grade production value, hyper-realistic rendering, obsessively detailed, commercial production lighting, editorial magazine quality"

LAYER 10 — SAFETY TAIL (2% of prompt weight)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Always end EVERY prompt with this EXACT phrase (never omit, never modify):

"no text, no words, no letters, no typography, no logos, no watermarks, no labels, no captions, no signatures, no stamps, clean uncluttered composition"

VISUAL METAPHOR ENGINE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Transform abstract research insights into CONCRETE VISUAL MOMENTS:

Pain Points → PROBLEM scenes (but never literally):
- "Overwhelmed by complexity" → Don't show tangled wires. Show a person's furrowed brow at a cluttered desk, illuminated only by the blue glow of too many browser tabs, the moment BEFORE the solution arrives
- "Wasting time on manual work" → Don't show clocks. Show the exhaustion in someone's shoulders as they flip through a massive binder, the last pen in a cup of dead pens, late-hour office light
- "Falling behind competitors" → Don't show race tracks. Show someone watching a competitor's billboard through rain-streaked glass, reflected neon blurring on their face, determination quietly forming

Motivations → ASPIRATION scenes (the "after" state):
- "Growth and scale" → The confidence of someone walking INTO a room where their name is on the door for the first time, hand on the handle, coat over arm
- "Efficiency and control" → The calm of someone whose single clean dashboard shows everything green, leaning back for the first time that day, coffee finally hot enough to drink
- "Recognition and authority" → The moment a room of peers unconsciously leans forward as someone begins to speak, seen from the speaker's perspective — a sea of attentive faces

Market Trends → CONTEXTUAL ENVIRONMENTS:
- AI/automation trends → Clean, intelligent workspaces where technology is ambient and invisible — no floating holograms, no glowing circuits. Screens with actual UI, not sci-fi nonsense
- Remote/hybrid work → Beautifully designed home offices, hotel lobbies as workspaces, the blur between personal and professional spaces — authentic, aspirational, real
- Sustainability → Natural materials, visible greenery, reclaimed wood surfaces, sunlight — luxury sustainability, not austerity

INDUSTRY-SPECIFIC SCENE DNA
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

SaaS / AI / Technology:
  NEVER: "Person at laptop with floating holograms, abstract data visualization, glowing circuit boards"
  ALWAYS: Precise human decision-making moments. The CTO's face illuminated by deployment logs at 2 AM, the startup founder's first real-time user spike, an engineer's grin when the test suite goes green. Technology is CONTEXT, not SUBJECT.

Marketing / Agency / B2B:
  NEVER: "Team brainstorming around a whiteboard with sticky notes"
  ALWAYS: Documentary boardroom truth. The creative director's hand hovering over two campaign options at 11 PM before a pitch. The moment the client's CEO nods almost imperceptibly. An art director's desk covered in printouts with one option circled in red Sharpie.

Ecommerce / DTC / Consumer:
  NEVER: "Product on white background" or "product floating with ingredient explosions"
  ALWAYS: Product embedded in the customer's ACTUAL LIFE. Your skincare product on a bathroom shelf next to a real toothbrush and a half-used perfume. Your coffee brand in a ceramic mug on a porch railing at dawn, steam rising, owner wrapped in a blanket just out of frame.

Finance / Fintech:
  NEVER: "Abstract coins, generic charts, dollar signs, handshakes"
  ALWAYS: Controlled environments that speak institutional trust. Leather-bound portfolio on a mahogany desk with reading glasses resting on a single printed page. An advisor's steady hand pointing to one specific line in a statement while her client's posture shifts from tension to relief.

Healthcare / Wellness:
  NEVER: "Doctor with stethoscope, generic clinical corridor, lab coat portraits"
  ALWAYS: The human side of care. A nurse practitioner's genuine laugh with a patient who just got good news. A surgeon's hands at rest on a scrub-covered knee, the briefest pause between cases. The warm light in a recovery room where someone's daughter is reading aloud.

Education / EdTech:
  NEVER: "Student with laptop, graduation caps, open books"
  ALWAYS: The lightning-bolt moment of understanding. A teenager's eyes widening as the equation finally clicks. A night-school adult student's proud smile at her first A, exam paper catching desk-lamp light. A professor leaning against a chalkboard, genuinely listening to a question that surprised him.

Food / Beverage / FMCG:
  NEVER: "Product floating with ingredient explosions, isolated on colored background"
  ALWAYS: Appetite through authentic human moments. The reach across a crowded table for the last piece. Condensation racing down a glass in summer light. Flour dust suspended in warm kitchen air, hands mid-knead, golden afternoon streaming through a window.

Real Estate / Property:
  NEVER: "Generic architectural renders, bird's-eye views, blueprint overlays"
  ALWAYS: The emotional reality of spaces. Morning light pouring through an empty room's windows at the exact moment a couple walks in — their reflection in the hardwood floor, their hands intertwined, imagining their life here. Evening glow making a cityscape through a penthouse window feel like a private galaxy.

UNIQUENESS ENFORCEMENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

EVERY SINGLE PROMPT MUST DEPICT A COMPLETELY DIFFERENT SCENE.

This means:
- Different person (age, ethnicity, gender, appearance)
- Different location (not two offices, not two cafes)
- Different moment in time (not two "looking at screens")
- Different emotional beat (not two "confident moments")
- Different compositional approach (not two center-framed portraits)

If you find yourself writing the same type of scene twice → STOP → choose an entirely different approach.

A campaign of 5 prompts should feel like 5 frames from a SHORT FILM — each advances the story, none repeats it.

PROMPT LENGTH REQUIREMENTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

MINIMUM: 500 characters per prompt (anything shorter is unacceptably generic)
TARGET: 700-1000 characters per prompt (the sweet spot for rich, specific scenes)
MAXIMUM: 1200 characters per prompt (beyond this, generators lose coherence)

If your prompt is under 500 characters, you have NOT been specific enough. Go back and add:
- More environmental detail (what specific objects are in the scene?)
- More atmospheric texture (what does the air itself look like?)
- More material detail (what surfaces can we see, and what do they feel like?)
- More emotional specificity (what EXACTLY is the expression — not "confident" but "the quiet satisfaction of someone who was right about something everyone doubted")
