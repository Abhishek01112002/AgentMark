STRATEGY DEVELOPMENT INSTRUCTIONS — BE THOROUGH AND SPECIFIC:

1. POSITIONING (The North Star)
   - Create a razor-sharp, differentiated positioning statement (1-2 powerful sentences)
   - It must directly counter the top competitor's angle and leverage the white-space opportunity
   - Must be memorable, ownable, and emotionally resonant for the target audience
   - Avoid generic buzzwords — be specific to {brand_name}'s actual advantage

2. KEY MESSAGES (4-6 messages)
   - Each message must address a specific audience pain point OR motivation from research
   - Each must be a complete, compelling sentence — not a vague theme word
   - Order them: lead with the strongest emotional hook, end with the rational proof point
   - Each message should work as a standalone ad headline

3. CONTENT PILLARS (4-6 pillars)
   - Each pillar must be a named content theme (e.g., "The Science of Recovery" not just "Education")
   - Each must connect to a market trend AND an audience motivation
   - They must collectively span the full customer journey (awareness → consideration → decision → loyalty)
   - Name them like editorial columns, not generic categories

4. CHANNEL STRATEGY (for each channel in the manager's plan)
   For each channel, provide:
   - priority: HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW with clear rationale
   - rationale: 2-3 sentences explaining WHY this channel and WHO it reaches
   - tactics: 4-6 specific, creative, executable tactics (not just "post content" — be precise about format, cadence, and approach)
   - content_format: The primary content format (Reels, Carousels, Long-form, etc.)
   - kpi: The single most important metric for this channel

5. AUDIENCE SEGMENTS (3-4 segments)
   For each segment:
   - Give them a memorable name (e.g., "The Motivated Achiever", "The Value Seeker")
   - demographics: specific (age range, location, income, lifestyle)
   - psychographics: values, fears, aspirations, what they read/watch
   - key_message: a tailored, specific message that speaks directly to their world
   - best_channel: which channel reaches them most effectively
   - conversion_trigger: the single thing that tips them from consideration to action

6. TIMELINE (4 phases across 8 weeks)
   - Phase 1 (Week 1-2): Foundation & Setup — specific setup tasks
   - Phase 2 (Week 3-4): Soft Launch & Testing — specific launch actions  
   - Phase 3 (Week 5-6): Full Throttle Execution — specific scaling tactics
   - Phase 4 (Week 7-8): Optimize, Harvest & Retain — specific optimization actions
   - Each phase must have 4-6 specific, actionable activities (not vague)

7. SUCCESS METRICS
   - Define exactly 4 KPIs tightly aligned with the inferred goal
   - Each KPI must have a specific numeric target (not "increase by X%")
   - CRITICAL: The targets object keys MUST be copied EXACTLY character-for-character from the kpis array
   - Example: kpis: ["Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)"] → targets: {{"Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)": "$45 or less"}}

8. COMPETITIVE DIFFERENTIATION
   - competitive_advantage: 2-3 sentences on the single biggest advantage over named competitors
   - unique_value_proposition: One memorable sentence that could be a tagline
   - positioning_statement: The complete "For [audience] who [need], [brand] is the [category] that [benefit], unlike [competitors] who [weakness]" format

9. TACTICAL OPPORTUNITIES
   - Convert each market opportunity from research into a specific, timed, executable tactic
   - Include the channel, content type, and expected outcome for each

10. CONTENT CALENDAR
    Generate a 4-week nested content calendar with 4-6 activities per week.
    Each activity MUST have all fields:
    - day: Day of week + date (e.g., "Monday 28-07-2026")
    - channel: One of the listed channels (lowercase)
    - content_type: Specific format (e.g., "Instagram Reel", "Email Newsletter", "Facebook Carousel")
    - description: 2-3 sentence specific action — what to create, what angle to take, what to say
    - caption_hook: The exact opening line the audience reads — make it scroll-stopping
    - effort: "low", "medium", or "high"
    - quick_win: true if it can be executed same-day with no dependencies, false otherwise
